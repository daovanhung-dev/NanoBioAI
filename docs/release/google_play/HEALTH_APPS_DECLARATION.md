# Google Play Health Apps declaration preparation

This document is a factual preparation sheet for the Play Console Health Apps
declaration. It is not evidence that a declaration has been submitted.

| Shipped capability | Factual classification | Console/reviewer note | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Health profile, body metrics, meals, exercise and schedules | Wellness and self-tracking | User-entered data is presented for personal wellness planning; the app does not claim diagnosis or treatment. | Source-ready |
| Nabi AI chat, care guidance, plans and sleep summaries | AI-generated wellness guidance | Responses are bounded by safety prompts and backend validation; users are told to seek qualified care for urgent symptoms. | Source-ready; legal review open |
| Sleep Safety microphone monitoring | Safety/wellness monitoring | Starts only after the user enables the feature and grants microphone access; foreground notification is required. | Source-ready; Android device test open |
| Meal catalog and health-topic text | Curated wellness content | New plans/replacements use reviewer-marked catalog rows; unreviewed imported claim text is not offered and is suppressed in persisted-meal detail UI. | Source-ready; content/legal review open |
| Community, diagnosis, treatment or cure claims | Not shipped / not claimed | Community is Coming Soon. Do not describe the app as a medical device, diagnostic service, treatment or emergency replacement. | Listing review required |

Before submission, the product/legal owner must confirm the applicable Health
Apps category and any required organization/contact details from the actual
developer account. No medical certification or clinical validation is claimed
by this repository.
