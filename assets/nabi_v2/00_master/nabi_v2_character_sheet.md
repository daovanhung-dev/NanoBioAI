# Nabi v2 character sheet

## Identity

Nabi is a gender-neutral seedling spirit: a softly tapered pearly-ivory seed
body, a two-leaf mint crown, forest-green facial details and chest leaf, and a
single restrained aqua water-drop accent. It is a calm companion, never a
robot, person, superhero, or medical authority.

## Construction rules

- Full-body, front-facing three-quarter-friendly silhouette with a wide,
  rounded lower seed body and short soft limbs.
- Crown: exactly two rounded mint leaves growing from the top centre; no hair,
  antennae, metal, or headwear.
- Face: small dark forest-green oval eyes, minimal brows, and a quiet curved
  mouth. Keep emotion gentle; no rage, sobbing, sharp teeth, or warning alarm.
- Chest: one forest-green leaf with one small aqua drop. Do not add text,
  badges, UI, medication, or health claims.
- Surface: refined soft 3D matte, diffused light, no glossy plastic or hard
  cast shadow. PNG assets have transparent backgrounds.
