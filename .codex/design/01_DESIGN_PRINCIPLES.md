# Design Principles

1. **Answer first:** first viewport states what matters now.
2. **Expressive with restraint:** contrast one or two elements, not every card.
3. **State is a first-class design object:** loading, empty, error, ready, locked and stale/refresh states are deliberate.
4. **Health confidence over gamification:** progress encourages; it never shames or diagnoses.
5. **Nabi supports, never blocks:** contextual presence and copy, not decorative occupation of screen real estate.
6. **Adaptive rather than stretched:** compact, medium and expanded layouts reorganize hierarchy.
7. **Accessibility is baseline:** large text, reduced motion/transparency, contrast and semantics are designed from the start.
8. **Tokens before local styling:** feature code uses semantic theme APIs.
